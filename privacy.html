The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or payment details.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
  • Anything you save stays in your own browser, on your own device. We never receive it.
  • The one exception is the “Sharpen with AI” button, which sends the text of your prompt to Google to be improved.

Information stored in your browser

The Constructor uses your browser’s local storage — not cookies — to make the tool work. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser data or using the “Clear all” button on the site. We store:

  • Whether you have unlocked the tool, so you don’t have to re-enter the mat word on every visit.
  • A count of how many times you have used AI sharpening in the past hour, to apply the fair-use limit.
  • Any prompts you choose to save, and whether you have dismissed the review message.

Because this information is strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for, and is not used for analytics, advertising or tracking, we do not show a cookie consent banner.

The “Sharpen with AI” feature

When you choose to use AI sharpening, the text of your prompt is sent to Google’s Gemini API, which returns an improved version. This only happens when you actively click the button — building, saving and copying prompts all work without sending anything anywhere.

Please don’t paste confidential, sensitive or personal information into a prompt you intend to sharpen. That includes client data, employee details, financial records or anything covered by a confidentiality obligation. Once text is sent to a third-party AI provider, it is handled under that provider’s terms, not ours.

Google processes this text under its own terms and privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not store the text of your prompts on our servers, and we do not keep a copy of what you send or receive.

Technical information

When you use AI sharpening, our server briefly processes your IP address in memory in order to apply the fair-use limit and protect the service from abuse. This is not written to a database, is not linked to you as an individual, and is not retained. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and operational purposes, as is normal for any website.

Shared prompt links

If you use the “Copy share link” feature, the prompt is encoded inside the link itself. Nothing is uploaded to us. Anyone with that link can read the prompt, so please don’t share links containing anything private.
